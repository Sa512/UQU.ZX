## English

🚀 The upgraded second version of Mudhaker is here

Mudhaker is an Arabic-first app for Saudi university students and faculty. It brings schedules, deadlines, grades, absences and attendance into one place.

What's new:
📥 Blackboard deadlines sync automatically, no university password needed
📢 Section channel: announcements from the professor reach students instantly
✅ QR attendance that refreshes every 15 seconds
🔐 Face ID lock and a hardened backend
🔥 Share cards and a "Semester Wrapped" recap

This version is built, tested and running in preview. It isn't fully launched on the app stores yet. The official release on the App Store and Google Play is expected around January 2027.

Universities, student clubs and faculty members interested in early access: let's connect 🤝
